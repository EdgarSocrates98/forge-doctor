### Bug found by the new mechanism (backprop candidate)
`PyModuleIndex.from_facts` restored `FunctionInfo.name` as the dict key -
the *qualified* name (`<module>.load`) - so `_functions_named()` never
matched on cache-restored modules and producer propagation silently
under-derived `returns_df`/`propagated_names` on warm scans. Fixed:
`name=qualified.rsplit(".", 1)[-1]` on restore. This is why first sig runs
mismatched - worth folding into docs/specs as evidence the signature
approach works.
